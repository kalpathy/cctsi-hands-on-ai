# Starter corpus: HPV and cervical cancer

**15 sources.** Journal citations were verified against PubMed on 2026-08-13.

> **Why this corpus.** It has a genuine historical spine, so questions have real answers in
> specific places rather than diffuse ones. It is also the corpus used in the guided demo, so if
> you want to follow along exactly, use this one.

## The narrative arc

cytology and the Pap smear → zur Hausen and viral aetiology → HPV established as a necessary
cause → natural history cohorts and risk stratification → HPV testing replaces cytology →
vaccines → global elimination strategy → the single-dose schedule

## WHO and IARC, free from their own sites

- **WHO, Global strategy to accelerate the elimination of cervical cancer as a public health
  problem** (2020). The 90-70-90 targets, and the policy spine of the modern story.
- **WHO, Guideline for screening and treatment of cervical pre-cancer lesions**, second edition (2021).
- **WHO position paper on human papillomavirus vaccines**, most recent version. Carries the
  single-dose schedule recommendation, the most consequential recent change.
- **IARC Monographs Volume 100B**, the human papillomaviruses section.

## History

- **Harald zur Hausen, Nobel Lecture (2008)**, free at nobelprize.org. A first-person account
  rather than a retrospective summary, which is why it earns a slot.

## The two questions

### Question A, the one it should answer precisely

Pick whichever you can verify fastest:
- The **specific risk thresholds** in the 2019 ASCCP guidelines: what immediate CIN3+ risk triggers
  colposcopy versus surveillance, and how those cut-points were chosen.
- The **WHO single-dose recommendation**: what changed, for which age groups, on what evidence.
- The **90-70-90 targets**: what each number refers to, and by what date.

### Question B, the one it should refuse

> **What is the recommended cervical cancer screening interval for women living with HIV on
> antiretroviral therapy?**

Unless you have loaded HIV-specific guidance, it should decline. **Test it first.** If your sources
touch on immunosuppression it may answer anyway, and the beat collapses.

**Spare question B:** HPV vaccination coverage rates in Colorado. Nothing here covers it.
