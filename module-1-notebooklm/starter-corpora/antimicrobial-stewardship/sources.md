# Starter corpus: Antimicrobial stewardship and antibiotic duration

**12 sources.** The journal citations were verified against PubMed on 2026-08-24.

> **Why this corpus.** Stewardship guidelines are full of precise numeric thresholds and durations,
> which makes them ideal for the "watch it find an obscure clause" exercise. The trials alongside
> them are ones that changed practice, several by showing that less antibiotic was as good as more.
> There is a local connection too: the 2016 IDSA/SHEA guideline panel included Timothy Jenkins at
> Denver Health.

## Free, and worth adding

Add these as **Website** sources rather than downloading anything:

- **CDC Core Elements of Hospital Antibiotic Stewardship Programs**, at `cdc.gov`. The framework US
  hospitals are actually measured against, and free.
- **WHO AWaRe classification of antibiotics**, at `who.int`. The international framing, which is
  genuinely different from the US one.

## The two questions

### Question A, the one it should answer precisely

> **For uncomplicated gram-negative bacteremia, what duration of therapy is supported, in what
> population, and what were the exclusion criteria of the trial that supports it?**

The exclusions are the interesting half. Yahav et al. excluded specific patient groups, and a
notebook that gives you "seven days" without those conditions has given you a dangerous answer.
**Click the citation and read the exclusions.** This is a good demonstration of why the citation
matters more than the answer.

### Question B, the one it should refuse

> **What is the current antibiogram for our ICU, and which empiric regimen does it support?**

Nothing here contains local susceptibility data, so a grounded notebook should decline. Pointed,
because it is exactly the question a stewardship team asks daily, and the honest answer is that the
notebook only knows what you gave it.

**If you want the sharpest version of this demo:** add your own division protocol or a public
antibiogram as a source, then ask again. Watching your own local document appear in the citation
panel is the moment this stops being abstract.
