## No biosketch of your own

Run steps 1 and 2 on a PI you support. Write down what their record is missing, in the order
you would ask them to fix it. That list is worth sending them before their next submission.

You can find the problems. Only the PI can certify the biosketch: NIH requires it from each
person, not a delegate.

## The rule, once

**You sign it.** A draft from a chat tool is a draft. Check every citation, date and claim before
it goes anywhere near SciENcv.

**Highly Confidential**. Still no PHI, anywhere.
