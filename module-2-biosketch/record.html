Module Two  ·  The record

Your career record

Everything here stays in your browser. No account, no upload, no key. The only things that leave this tab are your ORCID iD and publication titles, sent to ORCID, Crossref and PubMed.

01

Start with ORCID

NIH requires an ORCID iD linked to your eRA Commons account, and SciENcv builds your Products list from it. What comes back is your real compliance status.

Rank first, then fill: only the ten that go on the form need identifiers.

03

Fill in what nothing can fetch

Most of a CV is written down nowhere a computer can reach, which is why it goes stale. Start with three sections. One entry per line, year first:

2019-2023 Associate Professor, Department of Radiology
2021 Best Paper Award, Journal of Clinical Investigation
2022-present Member, Data Safety Monitoring Board

Saved in this browser as you type. Download your record at the end to keep it.

05

Rank against a project

Paste your specific aims. Publications are scored by shared terms, and the matched terms are shown so you can see exactly why each one ranked where it did. No model is involved.

06

Take it away

Paste the prompt into CU ChatGPT Edu or Google Workspace Gemini. Then check every citation it gives you against the list you handed it. Anything else is a fabrication you were about to certify.
