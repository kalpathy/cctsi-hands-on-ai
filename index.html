02

How a module works

Session one · 60 minutes

Guided, then hands on

Roughly twenty-five minutes of demonstration, then twenty-five of you doing it on your own laptop with help in the room.

Every exercise has a baseline everyone finishes and a harder version for people who are already fluent. Both are normal.

Between · about 20 minutes

One small assignment

Specific, and small enough to actually do. For Module 1 it is a notebook on your own topic with one question it answers and one it refuses.

Session two · 60 minutes

What broke, and show and tell

The things that went wrong get fixed first. Then people show what they built, including the parts that did not work.

The round on wrong answers is deliberately part of it, and it is usually the most useful ten minutes.

03

Two rules, everywhere

Rule one

Know which tool you are in. CU classifies data as Public, Confidential and Highly Confidential, and HIPAA is a separate question on top of that.

NotebookLM is approved up to Highly Confidential and is a No for HIPAA. Papers, guidelines, protocols, your own drafts: yes. Anything with a patient in it: no.

The part people get backwards: Microsoft Copilot and CU ChatGPT Edu can take PHI, with approval from the data owner. NotebookLM cannot. We cover the full table in the first ten minutes of Module 1. The authority is CU's AI tool comparison, not this page, and it does change.

Rule two

Click the citation. If a tool tells you something and you cannot get to the source, you do not have an answer yet. You have a sentence that sounds like one.

04

Who this is for

No prior AI, coding, or data science experience is assumed, and nothing here needs to be installed.

The room is deliberately mixed: KL2 and TL1 scholars, early faculty, study coordinators and research staff, biostatisticians, and senior faculty. That is why every exercise has two tracks. If you already use these tools daily there is a harder version of each one waiting, and you will probably end up helping the person next to you, which we consider a feature.

What you need

  • A laptop, Mac or Windows, fully charged.
  • A Google account, CU-affiliated preferred. A CU Microsoft account for Module 3.
  • Current CCTSI membership. Open to CU Anschutz faculty and trainees.
  • Attendance at both sessions of whichever module you register for.
